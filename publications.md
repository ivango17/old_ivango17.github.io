# Publications

### Biology-guided learning enables interpretable annotation of single-cell methylomes

In this manuscript, we extend our classification tool, scMKL, to single-cell 
methylation data. We showed superior classification accuracy and how scMKL 
addresses the need for supervised methylation annotation methods.

**Manuscript**: [Under review]()

**GitHub**: [GitHub homepage](https://github.com/ohsu-cedar-comp-hub/scMKL/tree/main)

**Documentation**: [scMKL API](https://ohsu-cedar-comp-hub.github.io/scMKL/)

**Anaconda**: [Anaconda homepage](https://anaconda.org/ivango17/scmkl)

**PyPI**: [PyPI homepage](https://pypi.org/project/scmkl/)

**Citation**: **VanGordon, I.**, Kupp, S., Adey, A., et al. Biology-guided 
learning enables interpretable annotation of single-cell methylomes. Nature 
Methods Brief Communications. Submitted Oct 9, 2026. *Under review*.


### Unsupervised semantic segmentation models of region of interest identification

In this manuscript, we explored several semantic unsupervised image 
segmentation models to benchmark them and complile them into a tool 
researchers can use on histology images with the goal of coregistering them 
with MALDI images. 

**Manuscript**: [Journal of the American Society for Mass Spectrometry](https://pubs.acs.org/jamsef/article/37/5/1068/5139821/Unsupervised-Semantic-Segmentation-Models-for)

**GitHub**: [GitHub homepage](https://github.com/PNNL-HubMAP-Proteoform-Suite/UnsupervisedSegmentation)

**Trelliscope**: [Results masks](https://pnnl-hubmap-proteoform-suite.github.io/unsupervisedsegmentation.io/)

**Citation**: Degnan, D., Knight, B., Logan, L., **VanGordon, I.**, et al. 
Unsupervised semantic segmentation models of region of interest 
identification. Journal of the American Society for Mass Spectrometry 37(5), 
1068–1076 (2026).


### Interpretable and integrative analysis of single-cell multiomics with scMKL

In this manuscript, we present our python package, scMKL, using single-cell 
RNA and ATAC data. ScMKL is an interpretable supervised classification tool 
for single-cell multiomics that is scalable to the volume of single-cell data.

**Manuscript**: [Nature Communications Biology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-025-08533-7)

**GitHub**: [GitHub homepage](https://github.com/ohsu-cedar-comp-hub/scMKL/tree/main)

**Shiny Application**: [scMKL analysis](https://huggingface.co/spaces/scMKL-team/scMKL_analysis)

**Documentation**: [scMKL API](https://ohsu-cedar-comp-hub.github.io/scMKL/)

**Anaconda**: [Anaconda homepage](https://anaconda.org/ivango17/scmkl)

**PyPI**: [PyPI homepage](https://pypi.org/project/scmkl/)

**Citation**: Kupp, S., **VanGordon, I.**, Gönen, M. et al. Interpretable and 
integrative analysis of single-cell multiomics with scMKL. Nature 
Communications Biology 8, 1160 (2025).
